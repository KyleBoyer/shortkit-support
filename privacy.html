Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortKit ("the app," "we," "us," or "our"), helper actions for Apple Shortcuts, handles information when you use it.

Short version

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The app does not collect personal data and does not send any data to us. We don't operate any servers for it.
  • There is no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, ShortKit stores a small amount of data locally on your device:

  • settings you save with ShortKit actions or in the app
  • your preferences in the app

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app deletes it. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are governed by your platform provider.

Shortcuts

ShortKit actions run on your device inside Apple's Shortcuts app. Text, links, JSON and lists you pass to an action are processed on device and returned to your shortcut. Link tools do not make network requests, open the URLs or read your clipboard automatically. They are not stored unless you save them using the setting or counter actions, or the app’s Settings screen.

Third-party platforms

ShortKit relies on operating system and platform features such as local storage and the Shortcuts app. Those platform providers, such as Apple, may handle limited technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children

ShortKit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through a support request. See the support page for other ways to reach us.
